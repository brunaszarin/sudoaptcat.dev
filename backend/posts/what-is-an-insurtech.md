For those not yet familiar with the term, it might sound obscure — but insurtechs represent one of the most innovative and promising segments in today's financial market.

The word comes from combining "insurance" and "technology," referring to the use of tech-driven solutions to transform how insurance products are built, distributed, and consumed.

Brazil's insurance sector is going through a major expansion and holds one of the highest growth potentials in the world. According to the Global Insurance Potential Index (GIP), Brazil ranks eighth among countries with the greatest capacity for development in this market. Even with that positive outlook, insurance penetration in the country is still considered low — which points to a massive opportunity for companies that can build more accessible, smarter solutions aligned with how modern consumers actually behave.

That potential already shows up in the numbers. According to data from Brazil's National Confederation of Insurance Companies (CNseg), between January and June 2023 the Brazilian insurance sector grew 19.8%, generating R$145.1 billion in premiums. That result even surpassed the volume recorded in the second half of 2019 — before the pandemic — when the market reached R$144.7 billion.

The combination of a rapidly evolving market and a population that's still underinsured creates the ideal conditions for innovation. The real challenge is making insurance simpler, more personalized, and more accessible — removing historical barriers like bureaucratic processes, lack of understanding around products, and friction in the purchase itself.

## Where insurtechs come in

This is exactly the context where insurtechs step in.

By combining technology, data, and new business models, these companies are redesigning the customer experience — building more flexible products, automating processes, and expanding access to financial protection for millions of people.

## What I see day to day

The transformation of the insurance market isn't just a trend anymore — it's part of daily life for anyone following this space closely. Working on the insurance side at Serasa, I've watched firsthand how insurtechs are reshaping the way companies and consumers think about protection, credit, and risk management.

For a long time, buying insurance meant navigating complex, bureaucratic processes that felt completely disconnected from the digital experience people already expect elsewhere. Insurtechs exist to fix exactly that — combining technology, data, and new business models to create journeys that are simpler, faster, and more personal.

This shift matters even more in a market like Brazil's, where insurance penetration is still low and the growth potential is enormous. The opportunity lies in bringing insurance closer to people's everyday lives, with products that are more accessible and better suited to different customer needs.

Working close to this market day to day, it's clear how digital solutions are driving real change — from fully online policy purchases to genuinely new models like on-demand insurance, risk-based personalized products, and data-driven platforms built for smarter customer experiences.

Insurtechs represent a new phase for the insurance industry: one where technology and market knowledge move together to lower barriers, improve the customer experience, and make financial protection a bigger part of everyday life in Brazil.
